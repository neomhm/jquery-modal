# Model card - the Extractor 1.0.0

## What it is

A bidirectional transformer encoder (RMSNorm, SwiGLU, rotary positions,
no biases) with four heads: one BIO label per token (25 span types, 51
labels), the document type (11 types), the language (10 languages), and a
masked-language-model head used only in pretraining. It reads one chunk
of a business document and marks the facts in it. It never generates
text: every value it returns is a slice of the input chunk.

| model file | preset | parameters | trained on | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `extractor-pilot.pt` | pilot | 11.5M (d 256, 4 layers, 4 heads, vocab 32k) | CPU (4 cores): 810 pretraining steps in 58 min, then 1 fine-tuning epoch (4,627 steps) in 79 min | delivered; a usable test model |
| `extractor-1.0.0.pt` | full | 32.8M (d 384, 8 layers, 6 heads, vocab 48k) | GPU, about 6-10 h | built by `py build.py full` on a GPU (not built here: no GPU) |

## Intended use

Inside the PLAN: `ingest.py` cuts a business's documents into chunks,
the Extractor marks the facts, `verify.py` / `normalize.py` check and
normalize every value, and `profile.py` builds the business profile with
the source of every value. It is meant for folders of one business's own
documents: invoices, quotes, receipts, brochures and web pages,
registration extracts, financial statements, price lists, staff lists,
contracts, letters, terms.

Not intended for: documents in other languages than the ten; scanned
documents without a text layer (there is no OCR); personal data
processing beyond what the PLAN needs (private customers are never
labelled as counterparties, rule R9).

## Languages

Arabic (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Morocco), Chinese (mainland, Taiwan,
Hong Kong, Singapore; Simplified and Traditional), English (US, UK,
India, Australia, Canada, Nigeria), French (France, Belgium, Switzerland,
Canada, Morocco, Senegal), Russian (Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus),
Spanish (Spain, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile), Italian (Italy,
Switzerland), Hindi (India), Japanese, Korean. Seven locales (ar-MA,
zh-SG, en-NG, fr-SN, ru-BY, es-CL, it-CH) are held out of training and
measured separately (`test_locale`).

## Training data and licences

* **Synthetic documents** (all fine-tuning data): generated by the code in
  `gen/` from word lists, sentence templates and document layouts written
  natively for each language for this project (no translation, no text
  copied from the internet, no external model or API). Every label comes
  from filling a slot with a known value, so the labels are exact. The
  generator, its data and the generated documents belong to the project.
* **Wikipedia** (pretraining and the language head only): plain text of
  the 2023-11-01 dumps (`wikimedia/wikipedia` via the `datasets` library),
  licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 / GFDL. The pilot used about 10M characters per
  alphabetic language (4M Korean, 3M Chinese and Japanese). The text is
  not shipped; the build downloads it.
* **Faker** (MIT licence): part of the person names in the locales where
  it passes the checks of section 10.3 (see `DECISIONS.md`). Addresses
  come from the project's own lists.
* **phonenumbers** (Apache 2.0) is used to make and check phone numbers;
  **hijridate** (MIT) converts Hijri dates.
* Libraries used to train: PyTorch (BSD), tokenizers (Apache 2.0),
  datasets (Apache 2.0), numpy (BSD).
* The tokenizer and the weights are trained from scratch: no pretrained
  weights or tokenizers.

## How it was evaluated

On held-out synthetic data (new values; held-out layouts, sentence
templates and activities; held-out locales), on trap-dense documents, on
100 hand-written chunks (10 per language) in styles the generator never
produces, and end to end at folder level (documents.db → extract_db →
verify → profile, compared with the truth of each folder). The full
numbers are in `REPORT.md` and `runs/<preset>/eval_tables.md`.

## Scores

See `REPORT.md` (section 5) for the measured values of the pilot model,
line by line against the targets of the build instructions. The pilot is
a small CPU model meant for finding data problems; the gate applies to
the full model only.

## Limits

* The training data is synthetic. Real documents will contain phrasings,
  layouts and errors it has never seen; the hand-written set gives a first
  idea of the gap, `real_eval/` measures it once real labelled chunks
  exist.
* No OCR: scanned PDFs give no text to `ingest.py`.
* Arabic PDFs may come out of pdfplumber in visual (reversed) order.
* Hindi PDFs with legacy fonts may garble the vowel signs (matras).
* Only the ten languages; the language head always names one of them.
* Scores are calibrated on synthetic dev data; on real documents a score
  of 0.9 may be less reliable than 0.9.
* The profile builder chooses one business per folder; a folder mixing
  several unrelated businesses gives a profile of the one best supported.
